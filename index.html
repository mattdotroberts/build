How do we make it easier for users to migrate from complicit tech to ethical alternatives?

// The problem

One challenge, presented by Tech for Palestine. 100 people: builders, creatives, strategists, anyone who wants to take action. Boycott lists contain thousands of products, but ethical alternatives may not exist for every complicit company, and switching can feel daunting to regular users.

Two ways in. Build an ethical, privacy-preserving alternative to a complicit technology, or migrate: where good alternatives exist, help tens of thousands of users switch through automation, integrations, content and advocacy.

// What you'll pitch

A working prototype or campaign, plus a full strategy for validation, go-to-market and growth. A software platform, a browser plugin, a migration guide, a data export tool, an advocacy campaign: what matters is that it addresses the challenge head on. The best projects are supported by the Tech for Palestine Incubator after the event.

Example targets. Stripe, Shopify, PayPal, WhatsApp, Spotify, Wix, Figma, Vercel, Microsoft, Instagram, Google. Five minutes per team at demo time: problem and goal, target users, working prototype, go-to-market, growth.

About Tech for Palestine

Tech for Palestine is first and foremost an incubator for advocacy projects. It supports anyone with a meaningful idea: working against media censorship, using tech to aid protests, scaling boycotts, pushing for tech divestment, supporting Palestinian entrepreneurs, and more. To date it has supported over 90 projects, each one contributing to a free Palestine in its own way.
